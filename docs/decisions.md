| D48 | 2026-10-21 | Floor rules: while a usable estimate (confidence ≥ `floor_min_confidence` 0.45, real mm, not at a ceiling) is below `min_width_mm` (1.30) and narrower than the target, Auto acts inside the noise band and below `min_confidence`; held 20 s after the last such estimate while under 1.35 mm; such decisions say "floor rules: ". Steps stay at `max_step` by default (`floor_step_factor` 1.0, at most 2, deploy override `CONTROL_FLOOR_STEP_FACTOR`); a bigger step only when configured and only widening, checked again before the send. Persistence, bounds and spacing still apply | Cube test: on infill the band (2 × sigma) was 0.2–0.35 mm and confidence ~0.5, so a nozzle fault waited 265 s and a clog was never answered. Replay of the cube data: first action +58 s (nozzle) and +40 s (clog dip), the same with 1× and 2× steps. Bench at 3× frame noise (2 Hz, correlated, 20 seeds): clog fixed within 180 s 16/20 with 1× steps (19/20 with 2×, 6/20 without the rules). The price is a drift wide on noisy steady prints: dips below 1.30 trigger widening that narrowing (outside the band, persistent) does not undo. 30 × 10 min at 3× noise: mean bias +0.074 mm with 1× (+0.111 with 2×, +0.019 without), off target by > 0.10 mm 22 % of the time (52 %, 2 %), flow up to 130 % in the worst run even with 1×. Hence 1× by default. At walls' noise (1×) no cost: 17 vs 15 moves per 100 min. On the cube's pre-fault steady phase the replay widened once (per the review; unverified whether that dip was real) |

| D50 | 2026-10-21 | Test bench page (`test.html`) and operator tuning. The operator sets a zone around the target (`band_below_mm` / `band_above_mm`, 0–1 mm, default 0.05 each side, stored relative to the target so it moves with it, persisted with it): Auto holds inside it and steers towards the target outside it; the noise band (2 × sigma) still holds too, and the floor rules (D48) still act below 1.30 inside any zone. Per lever the operator can switch Auto's use off or make its step smaller (whole numbers, 1 to the configured step, never larger; at least one lever on; memory only like Auto). The send check reads that tuning from the control state, not from the controller. Whether a tuned lever does anything is judged on its configured step. The pipeline keeps 15 min of 1 Hz scalar rows plus the operator's events at `GET /api/trace` | Filip's ask: run tests by hand and watch the system work, with Auto keeping the width around a target in small steps. A zone tighter than the noise would chase noise (hence the guard). Bench: a clog with flow only at ≤ 3 % per command recovers in 8–10 commands (median ~4.5 min vs ~1.6 min default, 18/20 within 5 min). The review found that scaling the usefulness bar with the step let a melt-limited flow send near-useless commands, hence judging at the configured step |
| D51 | 2026-10-21 | Faster measuring and reacting. (1) Views the camera model never fitted (cam1:-135, cam2:-45) are added as *followers*: anchored on the square to the fitted views, their offsets are relearned per print against the fitted views and never set the level; followers alone and never anchored count half confidence. Extractor gates unchanged (width_config_hash too). (2) The dashboard's width averages 15 s (`DISPLAY_WINDOW_S`), the controller keeps 25 s. (3) Deploy overrides `CONTROL_PERSIST_S`/`CONTROL_PERSIST_ESTIMATES`/`CONTROL_KAPPA`; the device runs 5 s / 3 estimates / 0.7 (defaults stay 10 / 5 / 0.5). (4) The Test bench shows every frame's outcome and the printer model's prediction per lever | Filip: faster, fewer thrown-away measurements. 37–51 % of accepted frames were dropped for lack of a fitted view; with followers all of them count: cube estimate available 99 % of seconds (73 %), sigma 0.091 mm (0.134). A shorter controller window made Auto slower (the 2σ band widens faster than the lag shrinks); persist 5/3 + kappa 0.7 recovered faults 15–40 % faster on the bench (cube replay nozzle fault +52 s vs +66 s) at ~1.7× unneeded moves and ≤ 1 reversal per fault at cube noise; Filip chose "as fast as possible". The DDSP's speed-only choices were right on fast infill (melt-limited: flow 85 % did not narrow the bead); at speed ≤ 65 % it picks flow |
| D52 | 2026-10-21 | The operator sets Auto's reaction by hand on the Test bench: the walls limit `min_width_mm` (0.8–2.0 mm), `persist_s` (0–60 s), `persist_estimates` (1–20), `kappa` (0.1–1), `pin_s` (60–900 s), `min_layer` (1–50) and `floor_step_factor` (1–2). `POST /api/control {"settings": {...}}` merges per key, `null` returns a key to the deployment's value; kept in `control.json` across restarts (Auto still starts off); the controller rebuilds its config at its next step, and an invalid combination keeps the old one. `limits.defaults` shows the deployment's values, trace rows carry `min_width_mm` so the walls line steps over time. | D53 | 2026-10-21 | Auto may move two levers in one move, and judges usefulness before choosing. The planner scores every single-lever step and (`pair_moves`, on) every pair of levers each moved the way it helps; only moves whose predicted change reaches `min_effect_fraction` × kappa × gap count, and the cheapest of those wins (cost + residual + step + a headroom cost: moving into the outer `headroom_fraction` 20 % of a lever's range costs up to `headroom_weight` 10 at the bound; moving out is free). A pair goes out as two commands, the slower lever first; the second once the first shows in the readback (no fresh estimate needed), dropped if the first isn't sent, fails, settles alone, or a foreign change touches either lever; the pair settles once on the slower lever's effect and the ineffective check judges the pair as one. `then` on a decision names the second command; "no usable lever" now names each lever's best full step and the bar | Live 21 Oct 19:12-19:30: speed at its 60 % bound, flow +10 % predicted +0.007 mm, nozzle +5 °C +0.012 (both the asked flow and the melt rate cap the bead), together +0.078; the planner also shrank the nozzle step to +2 °C (step penalty) and then judged that too small: 179 "no usable lever" alarms with the target 0.12-0.33 mm away. Filip: change two values at once and keep levers inside their ranges |
